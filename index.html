PuroChile

A reconciliation protocol for AI agents

AI agents can read any number of sources in seconds; what they cannot do alone is reconcile them. PuroChile is a set of rules no step may skip: every figure is rebuilt from its parts, checked against what the official source published, and the difference declared when it does not match. It is developed at the CDIA, and Chile's public data is its first test.

research lines

The questionsthat occupy me

Artificial intelligence is not one subject but a set of pressures on things that already mattered: work, education, public argument, and who gets to build. These are the four I am pushing on.

01

Work and the labour market

What generative AI destroys, what it creates, and whether that difference shows up in Chilean data before it shows up in Chilean politics.

02

Narratives and the history of fear

Every wave of automation produced the same argument. Reading the debates of the Industrial Revolution alongside today's is how you tell what is genuinely new from what only feels new.

03

Machines that write and judge

What models prefer when they rank, decide and moralise — and how well any of us can still tell their writing from our own.

04

Agents and democratised capability

Multi-agent systems and small models putting advanced tools within reach of the firms and institutions that were never going to buy them.

AI-first

A team of agentsat the frontier of knowledge

I work AI-first. Artificial intelligence does not come in at the end to polish the prose; it is the team the research is done with. An orchestrator breaks the question down and specialised agents work it in parallel — they read, analyse, challenge and verify one another — until only what held up is left. It means working where knowledge is moving: with tools that did not exist two years ago, on questions nobody has closed yet.

session logillustrative
  1. orchestrator → reading

    Gather everything published on generative AI and employment in Chile since 2023.

  2. reading → analysis

    41 sources read. Three employment series are comparable with each other.

  3. analysis → critique

    Hypothesis: the occupations most exposed to AI are growing more slowly.

  4. critique → analysis

    What about the business cycle? Test it against unexposed sectors before claiming it.

  5. verification → orchestrator

    Two figures don't match their official source. Flagged, not corrected.

  6. writing → human judgement

    Draft ready: every claim with its source, every doubt in plain sight.

orchestratedelegatechallengeverifydecide

/ the judgement is still human; the scale no longer is /

industry & public sector

Bringing AIinto the room

A good part of the work is not code but conversation: sitting down with executive teams at Chilean companies and public institutions to work out what generative AI can actually do for them, what it cannot, and what it would cost to find out. These sessions run through the Faculty of Engineering and the CDIA.

/ the useful question is never whether it works, but where it pays /
